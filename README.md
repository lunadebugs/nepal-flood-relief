# Nepal Flood Relief 🇳🇵

A lightweight, open-source platform built in response to the **2026 Nepal floods** to make critical flood and emergency information easier to access.

## About

Severe flooding across Nepal has affected communities, damaged infrastructure, disrupted transportation, and created an urgent need for accessible and reliable information.

**Nepal Flood Relief** aims to bring important disaster information into one simple, mobile-friendly platform so people can quickly understand conditions around them and find official resources.

## Features

The project is being developed to provide:

* Real-time flood and river conditions
* Active flood warnings
* A map of affected areas
* Road and transportation disruptions
* Emergency contacts and resources
* Links to official rescue and missing-person resources
* Clear timestamps showing when information was last updated
* A lightweight interface designed for mobile devices and slower connections

## Data Sources

Whenever possible, information displayed by this project will come directly from official or established sources, including:

* Government of Nepal BIPAD Disaster Risk Reduction Portal
* Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM)
* Department of Roads
* Official government emergency and rescue resources

Community-submitted information, if implemented, will always be clearly distinguished from verified official information.

## Current Status

🚧 **Active Development**

This project is being built rapidly in response to the ongoing flooding in Nepal. The first priority is getting useful, reliable functionality online as quickly as possible rather than building a perfect application.

Features will continue to be added and improved as needs become clearer.

## Principles

This project prioritizes:

**Accuracy over speed of information.**
Unverified information should never be presented as an official warning.

**Accessibility.**
The application should remain usable on mobile devices and slower internet connections.

**Transparency.**
Information should clearly identify its source and last update time.

**Privacy.**
Sensitive information about affected or missing individuals should not be collected unnecessarily.

## Disclaimer

This is an independent open-source project and is **not an official Government of Nepal emergency service**.

During an emergency, always follow instructions and warnings issued by the Government of Nepal, local authorities, the Department of Hydrology and Meteorology, and other official emergency services.

## Goal

Technology cannot stop a flood, but it can make critical information easier to find.

The goal of Nepal Flood Relief is simple:

**Help people find reliable information and assistance as quickly as possible.**
